# In-App Privacy Notice (CR-02)

> **Status:** draft, waiting for owner approval. Replaces the consent screens ([consent-v1](consent-v1.md), [consent-v2](consent-v2.md)), which are retired.
> Shown in a dialog the first time **Start Scan** is clicked. "Don't show again" is remembered in this browser only.

---

### Before you scan

- The app uses your **MacBook's built-in camera** to take photos of you from the front, sides and back, plus a few face close-ups.
- The camera is **only on during the scan** and turns off when you finish or leave.
- Photos stay in this browser until you click **Generate 3D Model**. Then they are saved **only on this MacBook**, encrypted, and used to build your 3D model.
- Nothing is sent to the internet or any cloud service.
- The 3D model is an **approximation** of you. We don't reshape your body or replace you with an avatar. If a good model can't be built, you'll see why.
- The try-on is a **visual preview only**, not your size or fit.
- **Discard scan & model** deletes the photos and the model at any time.
- Turn **off** macOS video effects (Control Center → Video Effects) for a true-to-life scan.
